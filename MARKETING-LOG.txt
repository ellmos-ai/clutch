1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
clutch (German: Kupplung) is the model routing and orchestration engine of the
ellmos-ai multi-agent ecosystem. It provides provider-neutral, local-first LLM
routing across Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), OpenAI (GPT/Codex), Ollama
(local & remote), Claude Code, agy (companion-for-agy), and Kimi (Moonshot API & CLI).
Using an intuitive automotive driving metaphor, clutch analyzes task complexity
and purpose, selects optimal model gears (G1-G5) and reasoning effort (0-100%),
tracks financial and token-throughput budgets with a 4-zone fuel gauge (Tankuhr),
enforces persistent circuit breakers (Bordcomputer), and optimizes decisions
via auto-learning (Fahrschule).
